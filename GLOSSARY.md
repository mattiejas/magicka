# Driftwake

A shared-world adventure sandbox in which players decide where to go, and grow stronger by combining skilling with boss fights.

## Progress

**Skilling**:
Gaining levels by gathering or making things, as opposed to fighting.
_Avoid_: Grinding, professions

**Cooking**:
The skill of turning raw meat and fish into food at a cooking fire. Food can burn, less often as the level rises.
_Avoid_: Baking, chef

**Tier**:
One of five grades of material and gear, copper and ash being the first and adamantite and ebony the last. A tier is not a level.
_Avoid_: Rank, level

**Worn tool**:
A basic pickaxe, axe, fishing rod or sword below the first tier, sold cheaply in a shop so that a player can always start over.
_Avoid_: Starter gear, tier 0

## Bosses

**World boss**:
A boss that rises on a schedule and is meant for everyone present to fight together.
_Avoid_: Event boss, raid boss

**Mini boss**:
A boss that a single player can defeat, and that returns shortly after it dies.
_Avoid_: Elite

**Contribution**:
A player's share of the damage a boss still carries. It shrinks as the boss heals and is halved when the player dies; damage taken does not count.
_Avoid_: Participation, score

**Telegraph**:
The visible warning on the ground before a boss attack lands.
_Avoid_: Indicator, marker

**Unique**:
A named item that only a specific boss drops. It can be traded unless it is a trophy.
_Avoid_: Legendary, rare

**Trophy**:
An item that can never be traded, so that owning it proves its holder earned it.
_Avoid_: Bound item, untradable

**Collection log**:
A character's record of what bosses dropped for them and which trophies they earned. What they bought or were given does not count.
_Avoid_: Achievements, checklist

## Methods

**Relaxed method**:
A way to train a skill that needs little attention and gives less XP.
_Avoid_: AFK method

**Intense method**:
A way to train a skill that needs constant attention and gives more XP.
_Avoid_: Active method, click-intensive

**Glowing**:
The state of one rock, tree or fishing spot of a group that, for a few seconds, rewards whoever strikes it with extra XP.
_Avoid_: Shiny, active, lucky

**Big rock**:
A rock that never runs out and gives ore slowly.
_Avoid_: Infinite rock, AFK rock

**Fishing spot**:
Ripples on the water beside the shore where fish can be caught from the land next to it. After a few catches it moves to another place along the same shore.
_Avoid_: Fishing hole, pool

**Shoal**:
A fishing spot that never moves or runs out and gives fish slowly; the big rock of fishing.
_Avoid_: Big spot, AFK spot

## World

**Encounter**:
A short, unannounced event in the world, open to whoever is nearby.
_Avoid_: Random event, adventure

**Rich vein**:
An encounter in which an ordinary rock gives more ore and does not run out for a while.
_Avoid_: Bonus rock

**Genie**:
A spirit that now and then visits one player and gives a lamp.
_Avoid_: Random event

**Quest**:
An authored, linear series of steps with a reward.
_Avoid_: Mission, task

**Dungeon**:
A self-contained place to explore that can hold bosses or resources found nowhere else.
_Avoid_: Cave, instance

## Money

**Coin**:
The unit of money. One coin has a shop value of one.
_Avoid_: Gold, pieces

**Shop value**:
The worth of an item in coins, which every item has.
_Avoid_: Price, cost

## Death

**Death fee**:
Coins charged because a player died, based on shop value.
_Avoid_: Tax, penalty

**Grave**:
The place where a player died, holding the carried items they lost. Only its owner can open it, and it never expires.
_Avoid_: Corpse, tombstone

**Reaper**:
The figure in each town from whom a player can reclaim what their grave holds.
_Avoid_: Death, banker

## Sea

**Sea**:
The map on which boats sail, holding the island, the mainland and small islands, with water between them.
_Avoid_: Ocean, overworld

**Boat**:
A player's own vessel, repaired once at the island's dock and then sailed by its owner.
_Avoid_: Ship, ferry

**Sail setting**:
How much sail is set: backing, stopped, half sail or full sail. Each brings the boat to its own speed in the end, slowly, since a boat carries way.
_Avoid_: Gear, throttle

**Course**:
A point on the sea the helm steers straight for, until the boat reaches it. There is no pathfinding at sea.
_Avoid_: Waypoint, destination

**Hard grounding**:
Hitting land fast enough to run hard aground. Every hit stops the boat; backing gets it off.
_Avoid_: Crash, collision

**Going ashore**:
Stepping off a slow boat onto land close beside it. The boat stays moored where it was left.
_Avoid_: Landing, docking, disembarking

**Moored**:
Said of a boat its owner has gone ashore from, where it lies until they board it again. A boat that has never left lies at the dock.
_Avoid_: Parked, anchored

**Place**:
A named piece of land on the sea: the island, the mainland or a small island.
_Avoid_: Location, zone

**Lookout**:
How far a player sees on the sea: 10 tiles, from the boat or on foot.
_Avoid_: Vision, sight range

**Fog**:
The parts of the sea a player has not yet seen.
_Avoid_: Fog of war, darkness

**Chart**:
A player's own record of the sea: every tile they have seen, and the outline of every place they have sighted. What is on a place shows only where it was seen up close, or all over once they have gone ashore on it.
_Avoid_: Map, minimap
