Sailing prototype (throwaway, issue #109)

Set your sail, then click where to steer. The boat gathers speed slowly, turns in an arc and carries way when you stop, so plan your turns and take in sail before the shore. Back the sail to go astern. Come in slowly beside land to go ashore. The sea starts in fog; sail out to find the islands and see what each one holds.

Sail:
Click to steer for a spot · WS or ↑↓ raise and lower the sail · AD or ←→ steer by hand · 1–4 back, stop, half, full

Chart

Only what you have seen. Click it to steer for a far spot.
